IMPORTANT — YOUR RIGHT TO CANCEL: a contract for work concluded away from the trader's business premises, which includes a contract signed in the customer's own home, is an off-premises contract under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013. A CONSUMER HAS FOURTEEN DAYS FROM CONCLUDING THE CONTRACT TO CANCEL IT WITHOUT GIVING ANY REASON, and the trader is required to provide the prescribed cancellation information in writing on paper or, with the consumer's agreement, in another durable medium. WHERE THAT INFORMATION IS NOT PROVIDED, THE CANCELLATION PERIOD IS EXTENDED BY TWELVE MONTHS. The company provides the prescribed notice in writing with every quotation accepted away from its premises, before any deposit is taken. Where a consumer expressly requests in writing that work begin within the cancellation period, and the service is then fully performed, the right to cancel is lost in respect of that performed service; where work has begun but is not complete, the consumer may still cancel and pay for what has been supplied. DEPOSITS: the company does not require a deposit disproportionate to the work or to materials to be ordered, and states what any deposit covers. BUILDING REGULATIONS AND PLANNING: work requiring Building Regulations approval is notified and inspected as the regime requires, and the company works to obtain the completion certificate and hand it to the client. Planning permission, where required, is the client's responsibility to obtain and work requiring it is not started before it is granted. REPLACEMENT WINDOWS AND DOORS: replacement of windows or external doors must either be notified to and inspected by the local authority's building control or be certified by an installer registered with a competent person scheme, and the resulting certificate is what a purchaser's conveyancer will later require; the company's registration is held under [COMPETENT PERSON SCHEME AND REGISTRATION NUMBER] and the certificate is passed to the client. RESERVED AND NOTIFIABLE WORK: notifiable electrical installation work is carried out only through a person registered with a competent person scheme under Part P of the Building Regulations and the certificate is provided; ALL GAS WORK IS CARRIED OUT ONLY BY A GAS SAFE REGISTERED ENGINEER and the company does not undertake gas work itself. ASBESTOS: in buildings constructed before the year 2000 an asbestos survey is obtained before intrusive work, work stops if suspected asbestos-containing material is found, and licensable removal is referred exclusively to a contractor licensed by the Health and Safety Executive. CONSTRUCTION SAFETY: where the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 apply, principal contractor duties are performed and a construction phase plan prepared. CONSUMER RIGHTS: the Consumer Rights Act 2015 requires services to be performed with reasonable care and skill and goods supplied to be of satisfactory quality.
