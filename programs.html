Program 01 · Permission before ordering

Building projects

We do not start work needing planning permission before it is granted, and Building Regulations notification is handled as part of the job with the completion certificate obtained at the end. That certificate is what a buyer's conveyancer asks for years later, and the moment it is missed is rarely the moment it hurts.

Program 02 · Registered where required

Installation work

Replacement windows need either building control inspection or a competent person scheme certificate, and homeowners usually discover the gap when selling. Notifiable electrical work goes through a Part P registered person with the certificate provided, and all gas work to a Gas Safe engineer. We coordinate rather than improvise around any of it.

Program 03 · Substrate first

Completion and finishing

Finishes fail from underneath. A screed still drying, a wall with unresolved damp, a substrate that is not flat enough: cover any of those and the finish fails within a year regardless of the quality of the work on top. We measure and wait where waiting is the answer, which is unglamorous and occasionally unpopular.

Program 04 · And what we refer

Specialist work

In anything built before 2000 we get an asbestos survey before intrusive work, stop if something suspect appears, and send licensable removal to an HSE-licensed contractor. Gas goes to Gas Safe engineers. The list of what we refer is on the quote, because a contractor who says yes to everything is telling you something.
